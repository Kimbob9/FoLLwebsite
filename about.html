About us

Local volunteers, one much-loved library.

Our mission

The Friends of Longridge Library is a community group of volunteers. We exist to support Longridge Library, to help it reach more people, and to make it a welcoming place for every age and background.

We work alongside the library team, never instead of them. Everything we raise and every hour we give goes towards making the library better for the people of Longridge.

The Friends at a glance

Volunteer-run and community-led.

Open to everyone: you do not need to be a regular library user to join.

Meeting regularly at the library. Ask us for the next date.

What we do

  • Book sales and swaps

    Donated books find new homes and the money raised goes back into the library.

  • Coffee mornings and community events

    Friendly get-togethers that bring people through the library doors, many for the first time.

  • Author talks and children's activities

    Story hours, crafts and visits from local writers to spark a love of reading.

  • Funding the extras

    We pay for the things that make a difference: from new books and games to comfortable seating.

Join us

New volunteers are always welcome, whether you can give an afternoon a month or just help out at the occasional event. No experience needed, just enthusiasm and a friendly face.
