Welcome

Hello, and welcome to the website of the Friends of Longridge Library. We are a friendly group of volunteers who believe a good library makes a town a better place to live.

Whether you love a good book sale, want to meet your neighbours over a cup of tea, or have a couple of spare hours to give, there is a place for you with the Friends.
